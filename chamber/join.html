NP Membership Benefits

  • No membership fee for registered non-profits
  • Listing in the chamber member directory
  • Access to monthly networking events
  • Quarterly chamber newsletter

Bronze Membership Benefits

  • Everything in NP Membership
  • Discounted entry to chamber training workshops
  • Two-event-per-year discount on premium events
  • Logo placement on the chamber sponsors page

Silver Membership Benefits

  • Everything in Bronze Membership
  • Quarterly spotlight rotation on the chamber home page
  • Free entry to two premium chamber events per year
  • Advertising in chamber newsletters
  • Priority access to chamber-hosted business clinics

Gold Membership Benefits

  • Everything in Silver Membership
  • Permanent spotlight position on the chamber home page
  • Free entry to all chamber events for two delegates
  • Featured advertising across chamber channels
  • Dedicated chamber account manager
  • Early access to government and trade liaison briefings
